# Scope Review — Working Draft

**Reference:** SR-03  
**Status:** Draft; comments unresolved

The original proposal described ECHO as a system for technical threat detection.
The expanded implementation appears to infer persistent patterns from activity
that may not be directly related to a specific security incident.

My concern is not that every correlation is inherently wrong. My concern is that
the system's purpose has become broader without a clear, auditable approval trail.

I recommended:
1. Pausing new behavioral-profile features.
2. Separating incident indicators from inferred personal context.
3. Recording a reason and retention limit for every collected field.
4. Restoring a documented way to disable expanded collection.
5. Completing an independent scope review.

I was told these questions would be addressed in the next review. I have not found
a final signed decision in the files available to me.
