# Repository Timeline

This timeline is assembled from the fictional repository's commit sequence and
the documents included here. It is not a complete account of the disappearance.

1. **Initial phase:** ECHO is documented as a technical threat-detection platform.
2. **Expansion:** Behavioral context and persistent scoring appear in project notes.
3. **Concern:** Youssef questions whether the expanded fields remain within scope.
4. **Proposed restrictions:** Youssef drafts a rollback plan and requests review.
5. **External contact:** Youssef exchanges messages with journalist Safa El Amri.
6. **Planned confrontation:** Personal notes indicate that Youssef wants to speak
   with CEO Bechir Masmoudi.
7. **Disappearance:** The case file records that Youssef is missing and may have
   been taken against his will.

## What this timeline does not establish

- Who kidnapped Youssef.
- Whether the missing review was deliberately removed.
- Whether Bechir knew about or participated in the kidnapping.
- Whether every proposed rollback was applied to a deployed system.
